Voice AI agents: live demos

Two voice agents that run in your browser with no account or API key. Chrome, Edge and Safari can listen; any browser can type.

  • Kitchen CompanionCook hands-free: say “Chef, next step”, set timers and ask how much flour goes in.
  • Interview CoachPractice an interview out loud and get a report on your pace, filler words and pauses.
