What the report measures

Pace
Words per minute for each answer, against a conversational range of 110 to 170.
Filler words
“Um”, “uh” and “er” per minute, plus habits such as “you know”, “like” and “basically”.
Pauses
Your longest silence inside an answer, and how many ran past three seconds.
Thinking time
How long you took to start answering after the question ended.

Answering out loud needs Chrome, Edge or Safari. You can type answers in any browser; the report then leaves out the timing measures.

In the full app, Claude Opus 5.5 also scores each answer on structure, specificity, impact, relevance and communication, and writes a coaching plan. This demo runs only the measurements.
