            Things to say

            Move through the recipe
            “Chef, next step”, “go back”, “repeat that”, “go to step four”
            Timers
            “Yes” when Chef offers one, “Chef, set a rice timer for 1 minute”, “how long is left?”, “got it” when it rings
            Amounts
            “Chef, how much flour?”, “Chef, make it for 8 people”
            Interrupt or ignore
            Say “stop” while Chef talks. Say anything without “Chef” and it's ignored as conversation in the room.
